# Papers still needed

16 papers whose full text would replace second-hand or abstract-level numbers with verified ones. Official titles and authors come from Crossref.

- **Tier 1** would improve the model or the validation but **none is blocking**: everything needed to start EBRT calibration is in hand, and LDR/HDR can start on second-hand values.
- **Optional** papers only turn second-hand numbers into first-hand ones. Each has a substitute already in hand, listed under it. Skipping them changes no design decision.
- **None is free to read.** The four free ones (Hwang 2018, Kubo 2021, Susil 2010, Song 2013) were retrieved separately, and Hamstra 2018 was supplied by the author; all are now read. The rest need institutional access or a copy from the author.
- None of these is in the local collection (a search of PDF contents found only textbooks and papers that cite them). None has been downloaded.

1. **[Tier 1]** Heikkilä VP, Kärnä A, Vaarala MH. **DuraSeal® as a spacer to reduce rectal doses in low-dose rate brachytherapy for prostate cancer.** Radiotherapy and Oncology 2014;112(2):233-236. DOI: [10.1016/j.radonc.2014.08.012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.radonc.2014.08.012)  
   *Why:* separation half-life 110 d vs gel-volume half-life 47 d (primary source for Vaggers' second-hand figures).  
   *Access:* paywalled.

2. **[Tier 1]** Beaudry J, Wang T, Aramburu Núñez D, Kollmeier M, Gorovets D, Enger SA, et al. **Quantifying geometric and dosimetric advantage from the use of rectal spacing in HDR prostate brachytherapy.** Brachytherapy 2026;25(1):198-205. DOI: [10.1016/j.brachy.2025.08.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brachy.2025.08.009)  
   *Why:* only HDR separation sweep (0.5-12 mm); HDR hold-out.  
   *Access:* paywalled.

3. **[Tier 1]** Kahn J, Dahman B, McLaughlin C, Kapoor P, Kapoor R, Harris E, et al. **Rectal spacing, prostate coverage, and periprocedural outcomes after hydrogel spacer injection during low-dose-rate brachytherapy implantation.** Brachytherapy 2020;19(2):228-233. DOI: [10.1016/j.brachy.2019.11.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brachy.2019.11.002)  
   *Why:* LDR I-125 dose vs separation (13.9 vs 6.5 mm); LDR calibration.  
   *Access:* paywalled.

4. **[Tier 1]** Agrawal V, Shah J, Shapiro MB, Alexander N, Kolm T, Keane J, et al. **Dosimetric comparison of implantable rectal spacers for low-dose-rate prostate brachytherapy.** Brachytherapy 2026;25(4):658-663. DOI: [10.1016/j.brachy.2026.02.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brachy.2026.02.005)  
   *Why:* LDR comparison of HA, PEG and balloon spacers; LDR calibration.  
   *Access:* paywalled.

5. **[Optional]** Nehlsen AD, Sindhu KK, Moshier E, Sfakianos JP, Stock RG. **The impact of a rectal hydrogel spacer on dosimetric and toxicity outcomes among patients undergoing combination therapy with external beam radiotherapy and low-dose-rate brachytherapy.** Brachytherapy 2021;20(2):296-301. DOI: [10.1016/j.brachy.2020.09.018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brachy.2020.09.018)  
   *Why:* LDR + EBRT, rectal V100 0.09 vs 0.17 cc.  
   *Access:* paywalled.  
   *Used instead:* Kwon 2026's forest plot already gives its V100 means and SDs (0.09 vs 0.17 cc, n = 22/146). LDR calibration already rests on Teyateeti, Taggar, Choi, Haddad and Butler.

6. **[Optional]** Wu SY, Boreta L, Wu A, Cheung JP, Cunha JAM, Shinohara K, et al. **Improved rectal dosimetry with the use of SpaceOAR during high-dose-rate brachytherapy.** Brachytherapy 2018;17(2):259-264. DOI: [10.1016/j.brachy.2017.10.014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brachy.2017.10.014)  
   *Why:* HDR (10 cc PEG) rectal V60-V80; HDR hold-out.  
   *Access:* paywalled.  
   *Used instead:* Armstrong 2021's Table 2 gives its V75, V80 and V40 values. The HDR hold-out already has Haddad, Chao, Ng and Lee.

7. **[Optional]** Koduri S, Barbee D, Oh C, Sherman T, Zelefsky MJ. **Interfractional Monitoring of Spacer Hydrogel Morphology in Patients With Prostate Cancer Receiving SBRT Treatment.** Practical Radiation Oncology 2026. DOI: [10.1016/j.prro.2026.05.015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.prro.2026.05.015)

8. **[Optional]** te Velde BL, Westhuyzen J, Awad N, Wood M, Shakespeare TP. **Late toxicities of prostate cancer radiotherapy with and without hydrogel SpaceAOR insertion.** Journal of Medical Imaging and Radiation Oncology 2019;63(6):836-841. 12. **[Optional]** Picardi C, Rouzaud M, Kountouri M, Lestrade L, Vallée JP, Caparrotti F, et al. **Impact of hydrogel spacer injections on interfraction prostate motion during prostate cancer radiotherapy.** Acta Oncologica 2016;55(7):834-838. ## Not requested

- The smaller EBRT cohorts in Kwon 2026's V70 plot (Cousins 2022, Khalil 2022, Navaratnam 2020, Yang 2020, Sagayanathan 2024): their relative V70 reductions are already recorded second-hand and are replaceable.
- Hama and Tate 2022 (J Radiosurg SBRT 8:105-8, quantitative MRI volume change of the spacer, ~20% water uptake): cited by Choi 2026; not in Crossref, so it could not be resolved.

## Corrections made while building this list

- An earlier-recorded DOI for Morita 2020 (10.1111/iju.14209) was the *Ghaffari letter replying to it*. Morita's paper is 10.1111/iju.14123.  
   *Used instead:* Armstrong 2021 and Kwon 2026 give its V100/V150 values. Only the prostate-deformation point is abstract-level, and it matters only for spacer-before-implant, which v1 does not model.
- Hamstra 2018 was supplied by the author and has been read. It contains **no rectal dose-volume data** (it reports penile bulb dose and sexual quality of life), so it did not provide the patient-level V70 SDs it had been listed for. Those now come from Mariados 2015 Table 2 and Song 2013.
